About

A private equity firm built to compound capital across cycles.

Bastion Bridge Capital is a value-oriented private equity firm. We acquire and build durable, cash-generative businesses through dedicated special purpose vehicles, and we hold them for the long term.

Philosophy

What it is worth, and what we pay.

We acquire control and significant minority positions in established, cash-generative companies, at prices that leave room for conservative financing and reinvestment. The question is always the same: what is the business worth, and what are we paying?

Our edge is not leverage. It is underwriting discipline, operational partnership, and a long horizon. We define the downside before the upside, and we build alongside management rather than around them.

The result is a firm built to compound capital across cycles, on behalf of investors who share our long view.

Transparent, aligned, deal by deal.

Approach

Control equity in businesses worth owning for the long term.

We concentrate on established, cash-generative companies where disciplined ownership and operational partnership can compound value over years, not quarters, each acquired through its own vehicle.

01

Control Buyouts

We acquire controlling stakes in established, cash-generative companies and take an active ownership role. Working alongside management, we implement operational improvements, sharpen capital allocation, and pursue strategic initiatives that build durable value, while mitigating downside risk through conservative underwriting and careful deal structuring. We buy to own for the long term, not to flip.

02

Growth & Minority Equity

Where control is neither available nor appropriate, we take significant minority positions in high-potential companies. We provide growth capital, strategic insight, and governance support, partnering with founders and management teams to unlock the next stage of the business while keeping their vision and leadership intact.

03

Founder Successions

Many of the best businesses are owned by founders and families weighing what comes next. We offer a respectful, lasting home: continuity for employees and customers, fair terms for the seller, and the patience to professionalize and strengthen the company without dismantling what made it work.

04

Buy & Build

We back strong platform companies and support a disciplined program of add-on acquisitions, expanding capability, geography, and scale over a long hold. Each add-on is underwritten on its own merits and integrated with care, so that growth compounds value rather than complexity.
